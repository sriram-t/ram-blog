Bookshelf

Books that shaped my thinking.

Designing Data-Intensive Applications

Martin Kleppmann

The single best resource for understanding distributed systems, replication, partitioning, and consistency models.

System Design Interview

Alex Xu

Practical walkthroughs of real-world system designs. Good for structured thinking about scale.

The Phoenix Project

Gene Kim

DevOps as a novel. Taught me more about organizational bottlenecks than any technical book.

Clean Architecture

Robert C. Martin

Dependency inversion, boundaries, and how to structure code that survives change.

The Staff Engineer's Path

Tanya Reilly

What it means to have impact beyond your own code. Technical leadership without management.

Database Internals

Alex Petrov

Deep dive into B-trees, LSM-trees, and how databases actually store and retrieve data under the hood.
